Grandma's Lemon Cake

Posted by Maria on a rainy Sunday.

This cake is bright, moist and ridiculously easy. I have made it every spring for ten years and it never fails. Below you will find exactly how I do it, plus a few notes about substitutions that work and some that definitely do not. Read the whole thing before you start, especially the part about the zest.

Ingredients

  • 200 g flour
  • 150 g sugar
  • 2 lemons, zested and juiced
  • 3 eggs

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Whisk eggs and sugar until pale.
  3. Fold in flour, zest and juice. Bake 35–40 minutes.

Serves 8.
