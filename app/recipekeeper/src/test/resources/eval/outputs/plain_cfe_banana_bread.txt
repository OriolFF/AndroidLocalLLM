Title: Banana Nut Bread
Description: This recipe results in what I feel is the perfect combination of flavor and texture in banana nut bread.
Servings: about 10 servings
PrepMinutes: 0
CookMinutes: 55
Ingredients:
- 2 large (250 g) ripe bananas
- 6 Tbs. melted butter
- 1 tsp. vanilla extract
- 2 large egg
- 1-1/3 cups (167 g) all-purpose flour
- 2/3 cup (130 g) sugar
- 1/2 tsp. (2.3 g) baking soda
- 1/4 tsp. (1.2 g) baking powder
- 1/2 tsp. (3 g) salt
- 1/2 cup (70 g) chopped walnuts
Steps:
1. Butter and flour a loaf pan
2. Preheat oven to 350°F (170°C)
3. Mash the bananas, melted butter, and vanilla extract together.
4. Lightly beat the eggs together.
5. Mash the banana mixture with the eggs until smooth and well blended.
6. Pour the banana mixture onto the dry ingredients.
7. Add the walnuts.
8. Fold the ingredients together until no more white flour is uncovered while folding.
9. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan and bake for 55 minutes at 350°F.
10. After 55 minutes, the loaf of banana bread should be done.
11. Set the pan on a wire rack to cool for ten minutes.
12. Remove the loaf from the pan and let cool on the wire rack.
Notes:
- Banana nut breads should always start with fully ripe bananas.
- Allow green or yellow bananas to ripen at room temperature until the skin is liberally covered with brown spots.
- When ready to use, simply thaw the bananas by letting them sit (unpeeled) on the counter until they warm up.
- The use of both baking soda and powder are necessary to provide enough leavening for the proper consistency of the bread.
- The baking soda is just enough to utilize the slight acidity of the bananas to create the desired carbon dioxide bubbles.
- Baking powder (which is a mix of baking soda, a base, and cream of tartar, an acid) provides even more leavening power.
- The loaf of banana bread should be done when a wooden toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
