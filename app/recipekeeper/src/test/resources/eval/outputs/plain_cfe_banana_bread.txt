Title: Banana Nut Bread
Description: This recipe results in what I feel is the perfect combination of flavor and texture in banana nut bread.
Servings: about 10 servings
PrepMinutes: 15
CookMinutes: 55
TotalMinutes: 70
Ingredients:
- 2 large (250 g) ripe bananas
- 6 Tbs. melted butter
- 1 tsp. vanilla extract
- 2 large egg
- 1-1/3 cup flour
- 1/2 tsp. baking soda
- 1/4 tsp. baking powder
- 2/3 cup sugar
- 1/2 tsp. salt
- 1/2 cup chopped walnuts
Steps:
1. Butter and flour a loaf pan
2. Preheat oven to 350°F (170°C)
3. Mash the bananas, melted butter, and vanilla extract together.
4. Lightly beat the eggs together.
5. Mash the banana mixture with the eggs until smooth and well blended.
6. Pour the banana mixture onto the dry ingredients.
7. Add the walnuts.
8. Fold the ingredients together until no more white flour is uncovered while folding.
9. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan and bake for 55 minutes at 350°F.
10. After 55 minutes, the loaf of banana bread should be done.
11. Set the pan on a wire rack to cool for ten minutes.
12. Remove the loaf from the pan and let cool on the wire rack.
Notes:
- Banana nut breads come in all varieties.
- The banana nut bread should be tender and flavorful, but not have the consumer feel like he needs to drink a glass of water with it.
- Drinking a glass of milk while eating a slice of good banana bread is an awesome combination.
