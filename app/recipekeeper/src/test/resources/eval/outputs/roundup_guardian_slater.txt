Title: Feta and potato pancakes with smoked salmon
Description: Soft, thick pancakes to top with sliced smoked salmon or fat prawns, soured cream and lumpfish roe, or to eat as they are
Servings: Makes 10 items
PrepMinutes: 15
CookMinutes: 10
TotalMinutes: 25
Ingredients:
- 250 g potatoes
- 150 g carrots
- 6 tbsp olive oil
- 2 eggs
- 200 g feta
- 3 tbsp dill chopped
- 3 tbsp plain flour
- 100 g smoked salmon or trout about per person
- salt and black pepper
Steps:
1. Peel the potatoes and carrots and coarsely grate them. Squeeze them with your hands to remove some of the water.
2. Warm three tablespoons of the olive oil in a frying pan, put the grated vegetables into the pan and cook them for seven to eight minutes until they have softened. Remove them from the pan into a mixing bowl.
3. Wipe the pan out with kitchen paper.
4. Beat the eggs in a small bowl with a fork or small whisk, then stir into the grated vegetables.
5. Crumble the feta into small nuggets (roughly the size of a hazelnut) and add to the bowl with a generous grinding of black pepper, then add the chopped dill and a little salt. Stir in the flour.
6. Divide the mixture into 10 and squeeze into patties about the diameter of a digestive biscuit.
7. Warm the remaining three tablespoons of oil in the frying pan, place the patties in the hot oil, press them firmly with a palette knife and let them cook for about five minutes till pale gold underneath, then tenderly flip them over and cook till light brown. Do not try to turn them over until they are golden-brown underneath.
8. Remove and set briefly on a piece of kitchen paper, then on to a serving dish. Serve with the smoked salmon or trout.
Notes:
- Soft, thick pancakes to top with sliced smoked salmon or fat prawns, soured cream and lumpfish roe, or to eat as they are
