Title: Feta and potato pancakes with smoked salmon
Description: Soft, thick pancakes to top with sliced smoked salmon or fat prawns, soured cream and lumpfish roe, or to eat as they are.
Servings: 10
PrepMinutes: 30
CookMinutes: 7-8
TotalMinutes: 38
Ingredients:
- potatoes 250g
- carrots 150g
- olive oil 6 tbsp
- eggs 2
- feta 200g
- dill 3 tbsp, chopped
- plain flour 3 tbsp
- smoked salmon or trout about 100g per person
- salt and black pepper
Steps:
1. Peel the potatoes and carrots and coarsely grate them.
2. Squeeze them with your hands to remove some of the water.
3. Warm 3 tablespoons of the olive oil in a frying pan, put the grated vegetables into the pan and cook them for 7-8 minutes until they have softened.
4. Remove them from the pan into a mixing bowl.
5. Wipe the pan out with kitchen paper.
6. Beat the eggs in a small bowl with a fork or small whisk, then stir into the grated vegetables.
7. Crumble the feta into small nuggets (roughly the size of a hazelnut) and add to the bowl with a generous grinding of black pepper, then add the chopped dill and a little salt.
8. Stir in the flour.
9. Divide the mixture into 10 and squeeze into patties about the diameter of a digestive biscuit.
10. Warm the remaining 3 tablespoons of oil in the frying pan, place the patties in the hot oil, press them firmly with a pallette knife and let them cook for about 5 minutes until tile pale gold underneath, then tenderly flip them over and cook till light brown.
11. Do not try to turn them over until they are golden-brown underneath.
12. Remove and set briefly on a piece of kitchen paper, then on to a serving dish.
