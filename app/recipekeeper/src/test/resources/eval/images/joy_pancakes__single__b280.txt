Title: Pancake Recipe
Description: A simple recipe for pancakes.
Servings: 1
PrepMinutes:
CookMinutes:
TotalMinutes:
Ingredients:
- 1 cup (130 grams) all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons (28 grams) granulated white sugar
- 1 large egg, lightly beaten
- 1 cup (240 ml) milk
- 2 tablespoons (28 grams) unsalted butter, melted
- extra melted butter for greasing the pan.
Steps:
1. In a large bowl whisk together the flour, baking powder, salt and sugar.
2. In a separate bowl whisk together the egg, milk, and melted butter.
3. Make a well in the center of the dry ingredients and then pour in the egg mixture, all at once, and stir (with a rubber spatula or wooden spoon) just until combined.
4. The batter should have some small lumps.
5. Heat a frying pan or griddle over medium high heat until a few sprinkles of water dropped on the pan or griddle splatter.
6. Adjust the temperature as needed.
7. Can also use an electric griddle with the temperature set at 350 degrees F (180 degrees C).
8. Using a piece of paper towel or a pastry brush, lightly brush the pan with melted butter or oil (or spray with a non stick vegetable spray.).
9. Using a small ladle or scoop, pour about 1/4 cup (60 ml) of pancake batter onto the hot pan, spacing the pancakes a few inches apart.
10. When the bottoms of the pancakes are brown and bubbles start to appear on the top surfaces of the pancakes (2-3 minutes), turn over.
11. Cook until lightly browned (about 1-2 minutes).
Notes:
- Do not over mix the batter or the pancakes will be tough.
- Adjust the temperature as needed.
- Can also use an electric griddle with the temperature set at 350 degrees F (180 degrees C).
