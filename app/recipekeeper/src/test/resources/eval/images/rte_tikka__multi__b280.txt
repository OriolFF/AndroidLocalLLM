Title: Chicken Tikka
Description: A recipe for Chicken Tikka.
Servings:
Cook Mode: Prevent screen from sleeping
Ingredients:
- 1 tbsp paprika (not smoked)
- 1 2/3 cups (400ml) tomato passata (tomato puree)
- 1 2/3 cups (400ml) water
- 100 ml (1/3 cup + 1 tbsp) cream (thickened or pure is fine)
- 1 tsp sugar
- 50g / 3 tbsp unsalted butter OR ghee
- Basmati rice
Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients except chicken in bowl and mix.
2. Add chicken and turn well to coat.
3. Cover with cling wrap and leave in fridge to
4. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a non stick pan over high heat until smoking. Add half the chicken and spread out. Leave for 2 minutes or until charred. Turn each piece and cook the other side until charred – don't worry if not cooked inside.
5. Remove into bowl.
6. Wipe skillet with paper towels (or do this part in a pot as you need a lid). Turn heat down to medium high.
7. Add oil and butter. When butter is melted, add onions, ginger and salt.
8. Cook, stirring constantly to ensure it doesn't burn, until the ginger is starting to turn golden and the onions smell sweet, about 5 – 7 minutes.
9. Reduce the heat to medium. Add the garlic and paprika, and cook for 2 minutes.
10. Add the Curry Sauce Spices, and cook a further 2 minutes, stirring.
11. Add tomato passata and water, and mix.
12. Bring to a simmer, then cover and reduce heat to low.
13. Simmer for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
14. Pour curry into a bowl, then use a stick blender to puree until smooth (Note 5).
Notes:
- Chicken: Please don't try this with chicken breast! The high temp cooking required to achieve the char grilled crust on the chicken will overcook it and dry it out.
- Garam Masala is a spice mix that is found in major supermarkets in Australia (Woolies, Coles etc) that is commonly used in Indian cooking. It smells like curry powder.
- Oil – Or canola, coconut, grapeseed or other neutral flavoured oil with a high smoke point. NOT olive oil.
