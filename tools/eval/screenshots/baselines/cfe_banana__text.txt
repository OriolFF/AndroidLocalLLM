Title: Banana Nut Bread
Description: This recipe results in what I feel is the perfect combination of flavor and texture in banana nut bread.
Servings: about 10 servings
PrepMinutes: 20
CookMinutes: 55
TotalMinutes: 75
Ingredients:
- 2 large (250 g) ripe bananas
- 6 Tbs. melted butter
- 1 tsp. vanilla extract
- 2 large eggs
- 1-1/3 cups (167 g) all-purpose flour
- 1/2 tsp. baking soda
- 1/4 tsp. baking powder
- 2/3 cup (130 g) sugar
- 1/2 tsp. salt
- 1/2 cup (70 g) chopped walnuts
Steps:
1. Butter and flour a loaf pan
2. Preheat oven to 350°F (170°C)
3. 2 large (250 g) ripe bananas mash mash until smooth fold
4. bake 350°F (170°C) 55 min.
5. cool 10 min. in pan
6. cool on wire rack
Notes:
- Banana nut bread should always start with fully ripe bananas.
- The use of both baking soda and powder are necessary to provide enough leavening for the proper consistency of the bread.
- After 55 minutes, the loaf of banana bread should be done. A wooden toothpick inserted into the center should come out clean.
