Title: Banana Bread
Description: Moist and delicious banana bread!
Servings: 8-10 slices
PrepMinutes:
CookMinutes: 150
TotalMinutes: 150
Ingredients:
- 100 grams sultanas
- 75 millilitres dark rum (or Bourbon)
- 175 grams plain flour
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- ½ teaspoon bicarbonate of soda
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 125 grams unsalted butter (melted)
- 150 grams caster sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 4 small very ripe bananas mashed (approx. 400g total weight with skins on)
- 60 grams chopped walnuts
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
Steps:
1. Put the sultanas and rum or bourbon in a smallish saucepan and bring to the boil.
2. Remove from the heat, cover and leave for an hour if you can, or until the sultanas have absorbed most of the liquid, then drain.
3. Preheat the oven to 170ºC/150°C Fan/gas mark 3/325ºF and get started on the rest.
4. Put the flour, baking powder, bicarb and salt in a medium-sized bowl and, using your hands or a wooden spoon, combine well.
5. In a large bowl, mix the melted butter and sugar and beat until blended.
6. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the peeled and mashed bananas.
7. Then, with your wooden spoon, stir in the walnuts, drained sultanas and vanilla extract.
8. Add the flour mixture, a third at a time, stirring well after each bit.
9. Scrape into a loaf tin (23 x 13 x 7cm / 9 x 5 x 3 inches) and bake in the middle of the oven for 1-1¼ hours.
10. When it's ready, an inserted toothpick or fine skewer should come out cleanish.
11. Leave in the tin on a rack to cool, and eat thickly or thinly sliced, as you prefer.
Notes:
- The banana bread can be stored in an airtight container in a cool place for up to 5 days.
- Can be frozen, tightly wrapped in a double layer of food wrap and a layer of foil, for up to 3 months.
- The rum soaked golden raisins become like juicy rum flavored morsels in this bread!
