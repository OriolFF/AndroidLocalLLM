FLUFFY BUTTERMILK PANCAKES
Serves 4Prep 10 minCook 15 min

Ingredients

1½ cups (190 g) all-purpose flour
2 tbsp sugar
1 tsp baking powder
½ tsp baking soda
¾ tsp salt
1¼ cups (300 ml) buttermilk
2 large eggs
3 tbsp (45 g) melted butter
1 tsp vanilla extract

Method

1Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt in a large bowl.
2In a jug, whisk the buttermilk, eggs, melted butter and vanilla.
3Pour the wet mix into the dry and stir until just combined. A few lumps are fine.
4Rest the batter for 5 minutes while a non-stick pan heats over medium heat.
5Pour ¼ cup (60 ml) batter per pancake and cook 2 to 3 minutes until bubbles form.
6Flip and cook 1 to 2 minutes more until golden. Serve warm with maple syrup.
Tip: don't over-mix, and keep finished pancakes warm in a 200°F (95°C) oven.
