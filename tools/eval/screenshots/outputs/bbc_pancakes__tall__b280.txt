Title: Plain flour pancakes
Description: A simple recipe for making plain flour pancakes.
Servings: 1
PrepMinutes:
CookMinutes:
TotalMinutes:
Ingredients:
- 100g plain flour
- 1 large egg
- 300ml milk
- oil or melted butter, for frying
Steps:
1. Keep the screen awake with cool mode on the Good Food app.
2. Sift the flour and a pinch of salt into a bowl. Make a well in the centre with the back of a spoon then break in the egg and pour in half the milk. Whisk together, gradually incorporating the flour to make a smooth thick batter. Beat thoroughly to remove any lumps, then stir in the rest of the milk.
3. Heat a little oil or butter in a medium frying pan, then dip the batter into the pan, tilting the pan as you pour, until the batter cools. Cook over a moderate heat for 30 seconds to one minute until golden brown on the underside.
4. If preparing in advance, cook and stack the pancakes.
Notes:
- You can store the made pancakes in the fridge.
- You can store the made pancakes in the fridge, wrapped in foil, for up to 2 days.
- Just reheat before use.
