Title: Chicken Tikka
Description: Chicken marinated in yogurt and spices, then grilled.
Servings: 4
PrepMinutes: 15
CookMinutes: 30
TotalMinutes: 45
Ingredients:
- 1 tbsp paprika
- 1/2 3/4 cups (400ml) tomato passata (tomato puree)
- 1/2 cups (400ml) water
- 100 ml (1/3 cup + 1 tbsp) cream (thickened or pure is fine)
- 1 tsp sugar
- 50g / 3 tbsp unsalted butter OR ghee
- Basmati rice
Steps:
1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a non stick pan over high heat until smold.
2. Add half the chicken and spread out. Leave for 2 minutes or until charred. Turn each piece and cook the other side until charred - don't worry if not cooked inside.
3. Note 4, also Video helpful here!
4. Remove into bowl.
5. Scrape out charred bits left in pan and discard. Add more oil if required and cook remaining chicken.
6. Wipe skillet with paper towels (for do this part in a pot as you need a lid). Turn heat down to medium high.
7. Add oil and butter. When butter is melted, add onions, ginger and salt.
8. Cook, stirring constantly to ensure it doesn't burn, until the ginger is starting to turn golden and the onions smell sweet, about 5 - 7 minutes.
9. Reduce the heat to medium. Add the garlic and paprika, and cook for 2 minutes.
10. Add the Curry Sauce spices, and cook a further 2 minutes.
11. Add tomato passata and water, and mix. Bring to a simmer, then cover and reduce heat to low.
12. Simmer for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
13. Pour curry into a bowl, then use a stick blender to puree until smooth (Note 5).
Notes:
- Chicken can be marinated for up to 48 hours in the fridge, or pop it straight into the freezer then it will marinate while defrosting overnight in the fridge.
- Don't be tempted to skip on the butter and oil for this recipe, it really affects the resulting richness of the dish at the end.
- You can use ghee instead of the oil/butter used to saute the onion.
