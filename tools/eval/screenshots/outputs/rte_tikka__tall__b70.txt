Title: Chicken Curry
Description: A simple and flavorful chicken curry.
Servings: 4
PrepMinutes: 15
CookMinutes: 30
TotalMinutes: 45
Ingredients:
- 1 lb chicken breast
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 can (14 oz) coconut milk
- 1 tsp curry powder
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1/4 cup water
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1/2 cup chopped cilantro
Steps:
1. Heat the oil in a pan over medium heat.
2. Add the onion and cook until soft.
3. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Add the chicken and cook until browned.
5. Add the diced tomatoes, coconut milk, curry powder, turmeric powder, garam masala, chili powder, salt, sugar, and water.
6. Simmer for 15 minutes.
7. Stir in the chopped cilantro and serve hot.
Notes:
- Use fresh ginger for the best flavor.
- Adjust the spice levels to your preference.
- This recipe is easily adaptable for different spice tolerances.
