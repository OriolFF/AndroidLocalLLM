Title: Feta and potato pancakes with smoked salmon
Description: Soft, thick pancakes to top with sliced smoked salmon or fat prawns, sour cream and lumpfish roe, or to eat as they are.
Servings: 4
PrepMinutes: 15
CookMinutes: 10
TotalMinutes: 25
Ingredients:
- 250g potatoes
- 150g carrots
- 150g olive oil
- 6 tbsp
- 2 eggs
- 200g feta
- 3 tbsp
- chopped
- smoked salmon or trout about 100g per person
- sour cream
- lumpfish roe
- or to eat as they are
Steps:
1. Make 10 pancakes
2. Mix the ingredients together
3. Cook the pancakes until golden brown
Notes:
- Soft, thick pancakes to top with sliced smoked salmon or fat prawns, sour cream and lumpfish roe, or to eat as they are.
- The recipe has been adapted for a more substantial dish with baked potatoes and a spoonful of garnet-red cranberry sauce.
