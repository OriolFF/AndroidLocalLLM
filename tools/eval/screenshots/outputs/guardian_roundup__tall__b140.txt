Title: Feta and potato pancakes with smoked salmon
Description:
Servings: 2
PrepMinutes: 20
CookMinutes: 20
TotalMinutes: 40
Ingredients:
- 500g potatoes
- 2 eggs
- 100g feta
- 100g smoked salmon
- 100g onion
- 100g dill
- 100g parsley
- 100g cream
- 100g flour
- 100g breadcrumbs
- 100g olive oil
- salt
- pepper
Steps:
1. Chop the potatoes into small cubes
2. Mix the potatoes with the eggs, feta, onion, dill, parsley, cream, flour, breadcrumbs, olive oil, salt, and pepper
3. Cook the mixture in a pan until golden brown
4. Mix the smoked salmon into the mixture
5. Serve with smoked salmon or tuna
