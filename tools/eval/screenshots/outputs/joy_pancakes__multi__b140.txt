Title: Pancake Recipe
Description: A recipe for pancakes.
Servings: 2-3
PrepMinutes:
CookMinutes:
TotalMinutes:
Ingredients:
- 1 cup (130 grams) all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons (28 grams) granulated white sugar
- 1 large egg, lightly beaten
- 1 cup (240 ml) milk
- 2 tablespoons (28 grams) unsalted butter, melted
Steps:
1. Heat a frying pan or grill on medium heat until a few sprinkles of water dropped on the pan or grilled spatula.
2. Adjust the temperature as needed.
3. Can also use an electric griddle with the temperature set at 350 degrees F (180 degrees C).
4. Using a small ladle or scoop, pour about 1/4 cup (60 ml) of pancake batter onto the hot pan, spacing the pancakes a few inches apart.
5. When the bottoms of the pancakes are brown and bubbles start to appear on the top surfaces of the pancakes (2-3 minutes), turn over.
6. Cook until lightly browned (about 1-2 minutes).
Notes:
- Pancakes can be made in any size and shape, ranging from the size of a silver dollar to a round as large as your pan.
- You will know it is time to turn your pancake when the top surface begins to form small craters.
- Pancakes are also made with buttermilk, which will never do.
