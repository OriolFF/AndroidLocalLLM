Title: CHICKEN TIKKA
Description: A recipe for chicken tikka.
Servings: 4
PrepMinutes: 15
CookMinutes: 30
TotalMinutes: 45
Ingredients:
- 600g/1/2 lb chicken thigh (boneless, skinless)
- 1/2 cup plain yogurt
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp salt
- cumin, ground coriander
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1/8 tsp cayenne pepper (optional)
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 6 cloves garlic, crushed or grated
- 1 tsp paprika (not smoked)
- 1/2 cup tomatoes (finely chopped or grated)
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter OR ghee
Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients except chicken in a bowl and mix.
2. Cover with cling wrap and leave in fridge to marinate overnight (3 hours minimum).
3. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a non stick pan over high heat until smoking.
4. Add chicken and cook until browned.
5. Add the sauce and simmer until about 5 minutes.
6. Reduce the heat to medium.
7. Add the garlic and paprika and cook for 2 minutes.
8. Add the curry sauce and cook for 2 minutes.
9. Add tomato passata and water, mix and simmer until the sauce thickens.
10. Heat to simmer for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
11. Serve over basmati rice.
Notes:
- Chicken can be marinated for up to 48 hours or for 2 days in the fridge.
- The dish is deliciously saucy because it's 50% tomato.
- You can use green tahini instead of the oil/butter used to make the sauce.
