Title: Pancake Recipe
Description: A recipe for pancakes.
Servings: 4
PrepMinutes: 10
CookMinutes: 10
TotalMinutes: 20
Ingredients:
- 1 cup (130 grains) all-purpose flour
- 1 ½ teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons (28 grams) granulated white sugar
- 1 large egg, lightly beaten
- 1 cup (240 ml) milk
- 2 tablespoons (28 grams) unsalted butter, melted
Steps:
1. Mix the dry ingredients together.
2. Mix the wet ingredients together.
3. Combine the dry ingredients and wet ingredients.
4. Cook the pancakes on a hot griddle until golden brown.
Notes:
- Pancakes can be made in any size and shape.
- Range from small to large.
- Cook until they are golden brown.
