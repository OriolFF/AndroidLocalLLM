Title: FLUFFY BUTTERMILK PANCAKES
Description:
Servings: 4
PrepMinutes: 10
CookMinutes: 15
TotalMinutes: 25
Ingredients:
- 1½ cups (190 g) all-purpose flour
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp baking soda
- ¼ tsp salt
- 1/4 cups (300 ml) buttermilk
- 2 large eggs
- 3 tbsp (45 g) melted butter
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
Steps:
1. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt in a large bowl.
2. In a jug, whisk the buttermilk, eggs, melted butter and vanilla.
3. Pour the wet mix into the dry and stir until just combined.
4. Rest the batter for 5 minutes while a non-stick pan heats over medium heat.
5. Pour ¼ cup (60 ml) batter per pancake and cook 2 to 3 minutes until bubbles form.
6. Flip and cook 1 to 2 minutes more until golden. Serve warm with maple syrup.
Notes:
- don't over-mix
- keep finished pancakes warm in a 200°F (95°C) oven.
