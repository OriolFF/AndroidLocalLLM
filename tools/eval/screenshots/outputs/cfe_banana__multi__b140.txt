Title: Banana Nut Bread
Description:
Servings: 1
PrepMinutes: 15
CookMinutes: 30
TotalMinutes: 45
Ingredients:
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 1/2 cup mashed banana
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 1/4 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup walnuts
Steps:
1. Cream together 6 T room temp butter, 1/2 cup sugar, add 2 eggs.
2. Mix in 1 1/2 c mashed butch cut, 1 1/2 baking soda, 1/2 cup walnuts.
3. Stir in 1/2 cup all-purpose flour, 1/4 tsp vanilla extract.
4. Stir to blend and add 1/2 cup walnuts.
5. Pour into buttered 8"x4" pyrex pan.
6. Bake at 350°F for 25 min.
7. Let it cool for 10 min.
Notes:
- If you need to flour the pan, sprinkle some flour, tap the pan so it is evenly distributed, then tap out the excess in the trash.
- If you have a lot of walnuts, you may want to use a little less.
- You can add other ingredients like chocolate chips or other nuts if you like.
