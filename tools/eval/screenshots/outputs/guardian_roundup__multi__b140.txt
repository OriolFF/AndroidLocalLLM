Title: Feta and potato pancakes with smoked salmon
Description: Soft, thick pancakes to top with sliced smoked salmon or fat prawns, soured cream and lumpfish roe, or to eat as they are.
Servings: 10
PrepMinutes: 15
CookMinutes: 15
TotalMinutes: 30
Ingredients:
- potatoes 250g
- carrots 150g
- olive oil 6 tbsp
- eggs 2
- feta 200g
- dill 3 tbsp, chopped
- plain flour 3 tbsp
- smoked salmon or trout
- about 100g per person salt and black pepper
Steps:
1. Peel the potatoes and carrots and coarsely grate them.
2. Squeeze them with your hands to remove some of the water.
3. Warm 3 tablespoons of the olive oil in a frying pan, put the grated vegetables into the pan and cook them for 7-8 minutes until they have softened.
4. Remove them from the pan into a mixing bowl.
5. Wipe the pan out with kitchen paper.
Notes:
- These are far from exclusive to Christmas eating, but nevertheless bring with them the spirit of the feast.
