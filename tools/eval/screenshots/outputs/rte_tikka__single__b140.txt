Title: Curry Sauce
Description: A simple and flavorful curry sauce.
Servings: 1
PrepMinutes: 10
CookMinutes: 20
TotalMinutes: 30
Ingredients:
- 3 tbsp (65ml) vegetable oil
- 30g / 7 tbsp unsalted butter OR ghee
- 1 onion, finely chopped (brown, white or yellow)
- 1 tsp salt
- cumin
- paprika (sweet, normal or smoky)
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp coriander
- 1/8 tsp cardamom powder
- 1/8 tsp cayenne pepper
- 6 cloves garlic, crushed or grated
Steps:
1. Heat vegetable oil in a pan.
2. Add butter or ghee.
3. Add onion and cook until soft.
4. Add ginger, garlic, salt, cumin, paprika, garam masala, coriander, cardamom powder, and cayenne pepper.
