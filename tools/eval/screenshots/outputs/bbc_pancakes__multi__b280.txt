Title: Pancakes
Description:
Servings:
PrepMinutes:
CookMinutes:
TotalMinutes:
Ingredients:
- 300ml milk
- oil
- or melted butter, for frying
Steps:
1. Sift the flour and a pinch of salt into a bowl.
2. Make a well in the centre with the back of a spoon then break in the egg and pour in half the milk.
3. Whisk together, gradually incorporating the flour to make a smooth thick batter.
4. Beat thoroughly to remove any lumps, then stir in the rest of the milk.
5. Heat a little oil or butter in a medium frying pan, then tip off the excess into a bowl.
6. Pour about 2 tablespoons of batter into the pan, tilting the pan as you pour, until the batter thinly coats the base.
7. Cook over a moderate heat for 30 seconds to one minute until golden brown on the underside.
Notes:
- You can store the made pancakes in the fridge, wrapped in foil, for up to 2 days - just reheat before use.
