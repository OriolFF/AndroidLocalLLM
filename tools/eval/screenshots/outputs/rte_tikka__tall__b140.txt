Title: Chicken Curry
Description: A simple and flavorful chicken curry.
Servings: 4
PrepMinutes: 15
CookMinutes: 30
TotalMinutes: 45
Ingredients:
- 1 lb chicken
- 1 onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 2 cloves ginger
- 1 tablespoon curry powder
- 1 teaspoon turmeric
- 1 teaspoon chili powder
- 1 teaspoon garam masala
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup water
- 1/2 cup coconut milk
- 1/2 cup chopped tomatoes
- 1/2 cup green chilies
- 1/2 cup chopped coriander
- 1 tablespoon oil
Steps:
1. Chop chicken into bite-sized pieces.
2. Heat oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat.
3. Add onion and sauté until translucent.
4. Add garlic and ginger and sauté for 1 minute.
5. Add curry powder, turmeric, chili powder, garam masala, and salt.
6. Cook for 1 minute.
7. Add water and coconut milk.
8. Add chopped tomatoes, green chilies, and coriander.
9. Cook for 10 minutes.
Notes:
- Use whole spices for a richer flavor.
- Adjust the spice levels to your preference.
- Serve hot with rice or roti.
