## Added by the lead (from `docs/plans/briefs/y1-report.md`, "Finding for the lead")
Also fix: `stepsCoverMethod` / `methodParagraphs` in `RecipeKeeperClipConfig.accept()` mis-fire on blog prose that has **no instructions heading** — they take the run of ≥ 40-char paragraphs after the last quantity line as "the method", which on `cronica.com.ar` is two unrelated paragraphs, so a correct 15-step model output fails the 60 % coverage check and the clip is rejected. Rule: apply the coverage check **only** when an instructions heading (built-in or learned) was found in the snapshot; otherwise compare against the whole readable text after the ingredient block and only reject when the parsed steps cover < 30 % of its words **and** there are fewer than 3 steps. Add a regression test shaped like the cronica page (ingredients, two short prose paragraphs, sub-heading, 15 method paragraphs) expecting acceptance.
